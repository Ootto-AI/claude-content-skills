# Series Planner — a format people come back for

Designs a recurring, named reel series — a repeatable format viewers come back for and follow so
they do not miss.

## What you get

- **A named format** — the name matters; it is what people refer to you by.
- **The repeatable shape** — the same opening, the same structure, so episode 9 is fast to make.
- **Twelve episode ideas**, so the series survives past the enthusiasm.
- **The follow reason** — why missing one would bother someone.

## How to run it

```
/series-planner I teach solo founders to use AI
```

## A series is a promise

Weekly means weekly. A series that skips is worse than no series, because it trains people not to expect you.
