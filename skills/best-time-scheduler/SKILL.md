# Best-Time Scheduler — a cadence you will actually keep

Recommends the posting times and weekly cadence that fit your audience's actual activity windows.

## What you get

- **Times from YOUR data** where available, not a generic infographic.
- **A weekly cadence** you can actually sustain — three good posts beat seven rushed ones.
- **Slot roles** — which slot gets your strongest piece, which is for testing.
- **What to change if it is not working**, and how long to wait before deciding.

## How to run it

```
/best-time-scheduler my audience is US-based founders, I can post 3x a day
```

## Consistency outranks timing

The right time for a post you did not make is irrelevant. Pick a cadence you will keep.
