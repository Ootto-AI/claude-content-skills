# Carousel Builder — one idea, ten slides, a save at the end

Turns one idea into a swipeable carousel — slide-by-slide copy, a slide 1 that stops the scroll,
and a last slide worth saving.

## What you get

- **Slide 1 that earns the swipe** — carries most of the performance, treated accordingly.
- **One idea per slide** — the most common carousel failure is three thoughts crammed onto a slide.
- **A save-worthy final slide** — a summary or checklist people screenshot, not just a CTA.
- **The caption** to run underneath, and where the CTA belongs.

## How to run it

```
/carousel-builder 5 mistakes killing your reach
```

## Length

8-10 slides. Fewer and it is a post; more and people stop swiping before the payoff.
