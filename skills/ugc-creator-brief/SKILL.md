# UGC Creator Brief — a brief a creator can actually shoot

Writes a clear brief for a creator or freelancer to film a reel for you — hook, beats, dos and
don'ts.

## What you get

- **The hook, written out** — not described. Creators deliver what you wrote, not what you meant.
- **Beat-by-beat direction** with the point of each beat, so they can improvise without losing the thread.
- **Dos and don'ts**, specific to your brand — the phrases you never use, the claims you cannot make.
- **Deliverables and format** — length, aspect, raw files or edited, and the deadline.

## How to run it

```
/ugc-creator-brief 30s reel about our onboarding, filmed by a customer
```

## Ambiguity costs a reshoot

Every vague line in a brief comes back as footage you cannot use.
