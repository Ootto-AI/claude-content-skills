# Audience Personas
Turn supplied customer evidence into 3 usable social audience segments. Do not infer demographics not in the evidence.

## Instructions
Paste the evidence, then ask: `Group this evidence into three audience segments. For each: job-to-be-done, language they use, objections, content angles, and the platform question they are already asking. Cite the source line for every claim.`
